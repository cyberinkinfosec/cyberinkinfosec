About

I'm Renjith RK,


An independent security researcher behind CyberInk InfoSec — a freelance security research practice focused on authorized penetration testing and vulnerability research.

My work is centered on offensive security, with a particular interest in black-box engagements. I approach targets with no prior knowledge and follow the same methodology an attacker might use — from reconnaissance and enumeration to exploitation and demonstrating real-world impact.

I don't just stop at identifying vulnerabilities. I follow findings through responsible disclosure, making sure security issues are properly communicated and, where possible, addressed by the relevant parties.

Alongside security research, I build my own tools and automation to make the work faster and more effective, primarily using Python, JavaScript, and HTML. I also use AI-assisted development as part of my workflow to prototype, automate, and iterate more efficiently.

How I work a target

Every black box engagement follows the same discipline, whether it's a client's production app or a personal research target.

01 · recon

Map the surface

Enumerate everything exposed — endpoints, subdomains, technologies — with no prior access or documentation.

02 · probe

Fingerprint & test

Identify frameworks, auth flows, and weak points; test each one for real exploitability, not just theory.

03 · exploit

Chain the impact

Turn individual weaknesses into a proven chain, showing exactly what an attacker could actually reach.

04 · report

Disclose responsibly

Document clearly, report through the right channel, and follow up until it's confirmed fixed.
